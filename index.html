Our Story

More Than a Shop —
A Living Tradition

In the bustling lanes of Raopura, Vadodara, a small but distinguished shop opened its doors in 1970. Patel Pen Centre was not merely a stationery outlet — it was the vision of one man who believed that a fine pen is the companion of every great thought.

Over five and a half decades, Patel Pen Centre has grown from a modest establishment into one of Vadodara's most trusted destinations for premium writing instruments — serving students, professionals, executives, and institutions with equal dedication and pride.

A pen is not just an instrument of writing — it is an expression of character, a mark of precision, and a bridge between thought and the world.

Through the changing tides of time — from the age of fountain pens to the era of branded ball pens and corporate gifting — Patel Pen Centre has remained the steadfast choice for those who value quality and authenticity.

The Founder

The Man Behind
the Legend

Navinchandra Chimanlal Patel
Founder & Visionary · Patel Pen Centre

Shri Navinchandra Chimanlal Patel founded Patel Pen Centre with a singular mission: to make the finest writing instruments accessible to the people of Vadodara. With an astute eye for quality and an unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction, he built a business rooted in trust, integrity, and a genuine love for the craft of writing.

His dedication to curating only the best pens — from everyday essentials to prestigious luxury brands — set the gold standard for pen retail in the region. His legacy lives on in every pen sold and every satisfied customer who returns year after year.

Our Journey

Milestones of
55 Proud Years

1970
Patel Pen Centre established by Navinchandra Chimanlal Patel at Siyapura Lane, Raopura Tower, Vadodara — beginning a legacy of fine writing.
1970s – 80s
Built a loyal customer base across Vadodara, becoming the go-to destination for quality fountain pens and ball pens.
1990s
Expanded offerings to include internationally renowned brands like Parker, Sheaffer, Cross, and Lamy — bringing world-class writing to local hands.
2000s
Forged partnerships with Gujarat's leading industrial giants for corporate gifting and bulk pen supply.
Present
Over 55 years of continuous operation, proudly serving corporates, government bodies, institutions, and individuals across Gujarat and beyond.
